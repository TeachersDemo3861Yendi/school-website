Effective Friday, 25th September 2026

📢 Important Notice to Parents/Guardians

Teachers' Demonstration Basic School, Yendi

Dear Esteemed Parents/Guardians,

We wish to inform you that, following the nationwide strike action declared by the teacher unions, effective Friday, 25th September 2026, teaching and learning activities at the school have been temporarily suspended until further notice.

Consequently, we kindly request all parents and guardians to keep their children/wards at home until the strike action is called off and the school officially communicates the date for resumption.

We sincerely appreciate your understanding, cooperation and continued support as we await an amicable resolution to the situation.

📌 Please do not send your child/ward to school until further notice.

Further updates will be communicated through the school's official communication channels.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Management
