Basic 5

Welcome to the Basic 5 class. Our programme focuses on developing strong foundations in literacy, numeracy and other important learning skills.

Subjects

English Language

Reading, writing, speaking and listening skills.

Mathematics

Numbers, basic operations, measurement and problem-solving.

Creative Art

Developing pupils' creativity, self-expression, practical skills, cultural identity, confidence, and appreciation of Ghanaian and other forms of art.

Science

Exploring the environment and developing basic scientific skills.

R.M.E

Helping pupils know what is right, develop good character, understand and respect religious and cultural differences, and become responsible members of society.

Computing

Preparing pupils to understand, use, create with, and responsibly interact with technology in their education, everyday life, and future careers.

Dagbani

Helping pupils communicate, develop literacy, preserve their culture and indigenous knowledge, and develop a strong sense of Ghanaian identity.

History

Learning about history of Ghana, our community and the wider world.

Learning Goals

  • Develop basic reading skills.
  • Improve handwriting and writing skills.
  • Develop basic numeracy skills.
  • Build confidence in communication.
  • Develop positive social behaviour.
