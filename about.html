About Our School

Teachers' Demonstration Basic School is committed to providing quality basic education in a safe, supportive and child-friendly environment.

Our History

Our school was established to provide quality education and support the holistic development of children. We continue to work with parents and the community to improve teaching and learning.

Our Vision

To become a leading basic school that develops confident, responsible, creative and academically successful learners.

Our Mission

To provide quality education through effective teaching, continuous assessment, discipline, creativity and meaningful partnership with parents and the community.

Our Core Values

Respect

We encourage pupils to respect themselves, others and the school community.

Discipline

We promote responsible behaviour and positive attitudes towards learning.

Excellence

We encourage every pupil to work towards achieving their highest potential.

Why Choose Our School?

  • Quality teaching and learning
  • Supportive learning environment
  • Qualified and dedicated teachers
  • Continuous assessment of pupils
  • Partnership with parents
  • Development of pupils' talents and skills
